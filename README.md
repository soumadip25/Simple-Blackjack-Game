# 🃏 Simple Blackjack Game

A lightweight, browser-based Blackjack game built using vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span). Draw cards, test your luck, and try to hit **21** without going bust[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span)!

🔗 **Live Demo:** [Play Here](https://soumadip25.github.io/Simple-Blackjack-Game/)

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Click **Start Game** to be dealt your initial two cards[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).
2. Click **New Card** to draw another card while the round is still active[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).
3. **Card Values:**
   - **Number cards (2–10):** Keep their face value[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).
   - **Face cards (J, Q, K):** Count as `10`[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
   - **Ace (A):** Counts as `11`[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).
4. **Win / Lose Condition:**
   - Hit **21** to get a **Blackjack**[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span)!
   - Go over **21** and you're out of the round[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).
