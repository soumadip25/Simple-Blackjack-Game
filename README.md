# 🃏 Simple Blackjack Game

A lightweight, browser-based Blackjack game built using vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Draw cards, test your luck, and try to hit **21** without going bust!

🔗 **Live Demo:** [Play Here](https://soumadip25.github.io/Simple-Blackjack-Game/)

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Click **Start Game** to be dealt your initial two cards.
2. Click **New Card** to draw another card while the round is still active.
3. **Card Values:**
   - **Number cards (2–10):** Keep their face value.
   - **Face cards (J, Q, K):** Count as `10`.
   - **Ace (A):** Counts as `11`.
4. **Win / Lose Condition:**
   - Hit **21** to get a **Blackjack**!
   - Go over **21** and you're out of the round.
